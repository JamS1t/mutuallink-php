Before you start

Who does what

Manager

manager

Approves memberships, approves/rejects and releases loans, closes accounts, manages loan products, user accounts, and settings.

Cashier

cashier

Registers applicants, receives membership fees, deposits, withdrawals, and loan payments; issues official receipts.

Loan Officer

loanofficer

Files, edits, and cancels loan applications, checks eligibility, updates the delinquency list, prepares reminders and demand letters.

Bookkeeper

bookkeeper

Corrects entries (savings reversals, voiding receipts), posts the savings interest, reopens closed accounts, prepares reports and the salary deduction list.

Board / Auditor

auditor

Read-only access to all records, reports, and the audit log.

Workflow guide

How the work flows

What happens in the system, in the order the work happens at the cooperative. For everything one role can do, see the role guides.

Section A.1

Signing in and the dashboard

Sign in with a username or email and password. After five wrong attempts, sign-in is locked for 15 minutes for that username on that computer (tracked in the database, so clearing the browser cookies does not lift it). The session ends after 15 minutes without activity; a warning appears first so you can stay signed in.

The dashboard shows the day at a glance: active members, the loan portfolio, today's collections, past-due loans, share capital, savings, applications in process, and installments due within 7 days. The tiles are ordered for the signed-in role (the Cashier sees today's collections first, the Manager the applications in process). It also shows a collections chart, quick actions for the role, the Manager's approvals & releases queue, the Loan Officer's delinquency summary, and (for the Manager, Bookkeeper, and Auditor) the most recent activity. The sidebar only shows the modules the signed-in role may use.

Section A.2

Membership Cashier → Manager

FFMPC's membership steps (questionnaire, Part II): pre-membership seminar (PMES) → membership form → membership fee and common shares → Manager's approval (about one month) → active member.

1 Cashier registers the applicant

Members → Register member. The TIN and PMES date are required, and the signature specimen card is ticked when received. Members from outside the school must later submit collateral for loans. The Manager may also register. If a member with the same name and birthdate is already on file, the system warns of a possible duplicate and asks you to confirm.

On saving, the system gives the member a number (here FFMPC-2026-0003), opens a Share Capital and a Regular Savings account, and sets the status to Applicant. The member record shows a checklist of what is still missing.

2 Cashier receives the membership fee

The membership fee is fixed at ₱250 (the Manager can change it in Settings). On the member record, click Receive ₱250.00 & issue OR. The official receipt opens, ready to print. Only the Cashier receives the fee.

3 Cashier posts the initial share capital (₱2,000 required)

Open the Share Capital account and post a deposit (see A.3).

4 Manager approves the membership

Members → Applicants lists everyone waiting for approval.

When every requirement is green, the Approve membership button is enabled.

After approval the member becomes Active, and the record shows the eligibility summary used for loans: good payment record, co-maker, collateral for members outside the school, existing loans, and share capital.

An applicant cannot borrow. Deactivating and reactivating a member never skips this approval step.
Section A.3

Savings and share capital Cashier · Bookkeeper

Each member can hold one account of each type: Regular Savings, Share Capital, Capital Build-Up (CBU), Time Deposit. Every deposit or withdrawal writes one line with its running balance and a receipt number, so the passbook and the office ledger are the same record. FFMPC's rules (questionnaire 3.1–3.6), enforced by the system:

Share capital required for membership₱2,000
Regular savings maintaining balance (and opening deposit)₱500
Time deposit minimum₱10,000 (or withdrawn in full)
Capital build-up₱100 per month (shown as a reminder)
Share capital withdrawalOnly with a BOD resolution (recorded on the withdrawal)
CBU withdrawalsNot allowed
WithdrawalsNeed the passbook (lost passbook: notarized affidavit of loss)
DepositsAllowed without the passbook

A withdrawal that would go below the maintaining balance is refused, and the message says how much can be withdrawn:

The passbook lists every transaction with its receipt number, running balance, and who posted it. The Bookkeeper corrects an entry with a reversal (↺); the original line is never edited or deleted.

1 Bookkeeper posts the savings interest

Savings Interest. Regular savings earn 1% per quarter; a time deposit earns 1% per term. Type the quarter label (e.g., 2027-Q1) and click Post quarterly interest. Every active account with a balance is credited in one run; accounts already posted for the quarter are skipped, and the database itself refuses a double post. Interest entries carry no official receipt. A time deposit is posted one account at a time, with its own term label. Only the Bookkeeper posts interest; the Manager can open the page but does not post money.

2 Bookkeeper corrects an entry with a reversal

On the passbook, click the ↺ Reverse button on the line and give a reason (e.g., a deposit posted twice). A reversing entry is added — the cash returns to the box on that day — and the original line stays on record, greyed out and marked Reversed. Nothing is ever edited or deleted. Lines without a receipt number (interest, the stockshare from a loan release) cannot be reversed.

3 Closing and reopening an account

An account at ₱0.00 can be closed by the Manager (passbook → Close account). A closed account accepts no transactions and can be reopened later by the Manager or the Bookkeeper, for example when a member who resigned returns. The passbook keeps every historical line.

Section A.4

Loan application, approval, and release Loan Officer → Manager

FFMPC's loan process (questionnaire 4.5–4.10): application with co-maker → credit committee evaluation → Manager's final approval → release in cash or check.
ProductLoanable amountTermInterest
Regular Loan₱5,000 – ₱1,000,000, up to 30% of the collateral's appraised valueUp to 12 months3% per month, diminishing
Salary Loan₱1,000 – ₱100,000; the monthly amortization must not exceed the monthly net payUp to 12 months3% per month, diminishing
Emergency Loan₱1,000 – ₱3,000Up to 3 months3% per month, diminishing

Rules checked on every application: the borrower must be an active member (an applicant cannot borrow); the co-maker must be another active FFMPC member, picked from the member list; a member may hold only one loan of each product at a time; and members from outside the school must give collateral.

1 Loan Officer files the application

Loans → New application → find the member. Enter the product, principal, term, co-maker, collateral (type, description, appraised value), net pay for salary loans, and how the member will pay. The eligibility summary is shown beside the form.

The amortization preview is computed live (equal principal, 3% on the remaining balance) and includes the semi-monthly amount for salary deductions on the 15th and 30th. The example is ₱50,000 over 12 months: 4,166.65 principal + 1,500.00 interest = 5,666.65 for the first month. Every monthly amount is rounded down to the nearest ₱0.05, like FFMPC's own computation sheet, and the last installment takes up the difference.

After submitting, the loan is Pending. While it is pending, the Loan Officer can still edit it or cancel it (with a reason).

2 Manager records the decision

The system records the credit committee's evaluation and the Manager's decision; it does not decide the loan. The person who encoded the application cannot approve it (separation of duties). A committee note is optional when approving; a reason is required when rejecting.

3 Manager releases the loan

Choose the release date and cash or check (a check needs its number). The deductions follow FFMPC's own "Summary of loan computation":

InterestNot deducted in advance
Loan insurance0.56% of principal
Service fee3% of principal
Stockshare2% of principal — added to the member's share capital
Notarial fee₱200
Others (printing)₱30
Previous loan (renewal)Remaining balance of an old loan, if selected; the old loan is then closed

Releasing generates the amortization schedule. The loan record shows the schedule, payments, amount currently due, full payoff amount, and details.

Schedule prints the member's copy, with signature lines:

4 Manager maintains the loan products

Loan Products. Each product sets the interest rate, the loanable basis (appraised value, net pay, or fixed amount), and the allowed principal range and term. Add product creates a new one; Edit changes limits and rates for future applications; Deactivate retires a product so it no longer appears in the application form (existing loans keep their terms).

Section A.5

Loan payments and corrections Cashier → Bookkeeper

1 Cashier posts a payment

Payments → Post payment → find the member → choose the loan.

Quick buttons fill in the amount due now, the semi-monthly amount, or the full payoff. The preview shows how the amount will be applied, in FFMPC's order: penalty → interest → principal, oldest installment first. An amount larger than one installment settles the next ones in advance, and paying everything closes the loan early. There is no rebate.

One official receipt lists every installment the payment covered:

2 After the loan term (past due)

Once the loan term has ended, the unpaid principal + unpaid interest is charged 3% interest + 4% penalty per month, computed per day (the monthly rate spread over 30 days). The example is an Emergency Loan of ₱3,000 whose term ended 62 days ago: ₱197.16 interest and ₱262.88 penalty have accrued, and they are collected first. An installment that is merely late while the term is still running carries no penalty.

3 Bookkeeper voids a receipt

Only the latest receipt of a loan can be voided, and a reason is required. The Void button is on the loan record, in the Payments list.

Voiding restores the installments, the balance, and the after-term charges exactly. The receipt stays on record marked VOID with its reason.

Any posted receipt can be printed again — from the payment list, the loan record, or the end-of-day close — either in the full report layout or in a narrow 80-mm thermal layout (Print (thermal 80mm)) for the receipt printer.

Section A.6

Delinquency monitoring and reminders Loan Officer

This is the part the bookkeeper said she would automate first (questionnaire 11.4). Delinquency → Update list now scans every schedule against the payments received, computes the days past due, and classifies each loan by aging bracket (1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91–180, 181–365, over 365 days). The list shows the member's contact number, the co-maker, and the portfolio at risk.

Demand letter prints a letter to the borrower listing the unpaid installments and the after-term charges, with a copy for the co-maker, since the co-maker is liable when the borrower fails to pay (questionnaire 7.4–7.5).

Reminders → Generate reminders prepares messages for installments due within 3 days (the number of days is a setting) and for overdue loans; a reminder that is still unsent is not created twice. FFMPC prefers text messages: the phone button opens the SMS app with the message ready, the envelope button opens the email app, and the Loan Officer then marks the reminder sent or failed, or removes it. Reminders can also go out by email automatically: once the Manager has filled in Email settings, a scheduled task runs cron_send_reminders.php and emails every pending reminder whose member has an address. A failed send keeps the reason on the reminder, shown in red.

The Manager fills in the SMTP account under Reminders → Email settings: host, port (587 STARTTLS or 465 SSL), username, the cooperative's From address, and a Gmail App Password (never the sign-in password). The password is never displayed again (only its last two characters, as a check). The installation README explains how to schedule the task.

Section A.7

Reports, audit log, settings, and access control

Reports

Every report can be copied, exported to Excel/CSV, or printed, and each role sees only its reports:

ReportWho can open it
Daily collectionManager, Cashier, Bookkeeper, Board / Auditor
Loan status · Delinquency & agingManager, Loan Officer, Board / Auditor
Savings · Share capital · Salary deduction listManager, Bookkeeper, Board / Auditor
End-of-day closeManager, Cashier, Bookkeeper, Board / Auditor
Reconciliation · Audit logManager, Bookkeeper, Board / Auditor

Daily collection supports end-of-day balancing: every receipt of the day with cash in, cash out, net, and totals per cashier.

Salary deduction list (prepared by the Bookkeeper): what to deduct on the 15th and the 30th for members who pay by salary deduction.

Share capital (Board / Auditor view), with each member's share of the total. The other reports are loan status and delinquency & aging.

End-of-day close and reconciliation

End-of-day close lists every posted receipt of one day — loan payments, savings deposits and withdrawals, membership fees, and reversals — grouped by type with the cash-box grand total, ready to print for counting the box. A reversal appears as its own Savings reversal line on the day the cash returned. Reconciliation is the integrity check: for every released loan, the schedule balance must equal the ledger; for every savings account, the transactions must equal the stored balance. Both pages are read-only.

The other reports

Loan status (portfolio by status with outstanding balances), Delinquency & aging (the same brackets as the delinquency list), and Savings (balances per account type). Every report exports to CSV/Excel or prints, and each role sees only its own reports.

Audit log

Every sign-in, entry, approval, correction, and settings change is recorded with the user and time. The log cannot be edited.

Settings Manager

Deduction rates, the collateral loanable percentage, account minimums, the membership fee, the savings interest rate, reminder timing, aging brackets, the language of the action words on buttons, and the official receipt series. Every role can open Settings to read the rules; only the Manager can change them. The Manager sets the last number issued to match the receipt booklet, and the series can only move forward. The panel on the right checks the rates against FFMPC's ₱26,000 sample computation.

User accounts Manager

Add, edit, reset passwords, and deactivate or reactivate staff accounts. Accounts are never deleted, so the audit trail stays intact; at least one active Manager must always remain, and you cannot deactivate your own account or change your own role.

Access control

A role that opens a page it is not allowed to use gets Access denied, even when typing the address directly. Shown here is the Board / Auditor trying to add a user.

Your password and logging out

Click your name at the top right: the menu shows your role, Change password, and Log out. Changing the password asks for the current one, and the new one (at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number, different from the old one) is typed twice. Your name, username, and email are changed by the Manager under User accounts.

Find any member from anywhere

Press Ctrl+K or / (or click Find member in the top bar) on any page: type a name or member number and jump straight to the record, with only the actions your role allows.

Role guides

Step by step, for each role

Find your role and follow the steps. Each guide covers everything that role can do in MutualLink, with the same sample members used in the workflow guide.

For every role: sign in (Signing in), find any member with Ctrl+K (Find any member), change your own password and log out from the menu under your name (Your password). Every role can also open Members, Savings & Share Capital, Loans, Payments, Loan Products, and Settings to read them; the steps below are what each role can do.
Role guide · sign in as manager

Manager

You can

  • Approve memberships; register, edit, deactivate, and reactivate members
  • Approve or reject loan applications, and release approved loans
  • Add, edit, and deactivate loan products
  • Open savings accounts; close an account at zero balance; reopen it
  • Change Settings and the reminder Email settings
  • Add, edit, deactivate staff accounts and reset their passwords
  • Open every report, the end-of-day close, reconciliation, and the audit log

You cannot

  • Post money: no deposits, withdrawals, membership fees, loan payments, or savings interest
  • File a loan application (Loan Officer)
  • Void a receipt or reverse a savings entry (Bookkeeper)
  • Decide a loan application you encoded yourself

1 Start from the dashboard

After signing in, the dashboard shows what is waiting for you: applications in process and loans awaiting release come first, and the approvals & releases queue lists each one. Click a loan number in the queue to open it.

2 Approve a membership

Members → Applicants. Open the applicant's record with the eye button.

Check that all five requirements are green (PMES, signature card, TIN, membership fee, ₱2,000 share capital), then click Approve membership and confirm. The button stays disabled while anything is missing.

The member is now Active and can borrow.

On the member list you can also edit a profile (pen), deactivate a member (red button; refused while the member has a pending or running loan), and reactivate one from the Inactive filter. You can register a member too (same form as the Cashier, step 2) and approve the membership yourself once the Cashier has received the fee and the share capital.

3 Decide a loan application

Loans → Pending, or the queue on the dashboard. Read the eligibility summary and the details, type the committee note if there is one, then click Approve and confirm. Reject asks for a reason.

4 Release the approved loan

On the approved loan, set the release date, choose Cash or Check (type the check number), review the deductions and the net proceeds, then click Release and generate schedule and confirm. If the member has another running loan, you can offset it here as a renewal.

The loan is now Released, with its amortization schedule. Click Schedule and print the member's copy.

5 Maintain the loan products

Loan Products. Add product, or Edit / Deactivate on a product card.

6 Close or reopen a savings account

Open the account's passbook (Savings & Share Capital → Passbook). At ₱0.00 the Close account button appears; confirm to close. A closed account shows Reopen account instead. You can also open a new account for a member from Savings & Share Capital → Open an account (see the Cashier, step 5).

7 Change the settings

Settings. Change a value and click Save settings. The panel on the right recomputes FFMPC's ₱26,000 sample so you can check the rates.

Reminders → Email settings (at the bottom of the Reminders page) holds the email account that sends the reminders. On the Reminders page itself the Manager can read the list; generating and sending is the Loan Officer's work.

8 Manage the staff accounts

User Accounts. Add user creates an account; Edit changes the name, role, or status, or resets the password; Deactivate stops a person from signing in.

9 Review: reports, reconciliation, audit log

The Manager can open all six reports (Reports), the End-of-day close, Delinquency (to read; updating the list is the Loan Officer's), Savings Interest (to read), Reconciliation, and the Audit Log. Reconciliation should always be green.

Role guide · sign in as cashier

Cashier

You can

  • Register applicants and edit member profiles
  • Receive the membership fee and issue its receipt
  • Open savings accounts; post deposits and withdrawals
  • Post loan payments and issue official receipts
  • Reprint any receipt (report or thermal 80mm layout)
  • Open the Daily collection report and the End-of-day close

You cannot

  • Approve memberships or loans, or release loans (Manager)
  • File loan applications (Loan Officer)
  • Void a receipt or reverse an entry: ask the Bookkeeper
  • Open Delinquency, Reminders, Savings Interest, Reconciliation, the Audit Log, or User Accounts

1 Start from the dashboard

Today's loan collections and deposits come first. Quick actions has the things you do all day: post a loan payment, post a deposit or withdrawal, register a member.

2 Register an applicant

Members → Register member.

Fill in the form. Fields marked * are required; the TIN and the PMES date must be given. Click Register member.

The applicant's record opens, with the checklist of what is still missing.

3 Receive the membership fee

On the applicant's record, click Receive ₱250.00 & issue OR. The receipt opens; print it and go back.

4 Post the initial share capital

On the member record, open the Accounts tab and click Open on Share Capital, then Post transaction on the passbook.

Enter the amount (₱2,000 is required for membership) and click Post and issue receipt.

The applicant is now complete and waits for the Manager's approval.

5 Open another account for a member

Savings & Share Capital lists every account. To open a Capital Build-Up or Time Deposit account, find the member under Open an account, choose the type, and click Open account. A member has at most one account of each type.

6 Post a deposit or a withdrawal

Click Post on the account (or Post transaction on its passbook). Choose Deposit or Withdraw, enter the amount, and for a withdrawal tick Member presented the passbook. A share capital withdrawal also needs the BOD resolution number. If a withdrawal would go below the maintaining balance, the system refuses it and tells you how much can be withdrawn.

The passbook shows every line with its receipt number and running balance. Click a receipt number to reprint it.

7 Post a loan payment

Payments → Post payment. Type the member's name or number and pick the member.

Type the amount received, or tap a quick-fill button. Check how it will be applied, choose the mode of payment, and click Post payment and issue OR.

Posted the wrong amount? You cannot change or delete a receipt. Tell the Bookkeeper, who voids the latest receipt of the loan; then post the payment again.

8 Receive a payment on a loan past its term

The screen is the same, but the after-term interest and penalty are shown and collected first. If the member paid on an earlier day, set Payment received on and click Recompute.

9 Look up and reprint receipts

Payments lists every loan payment. Filter by date, and click an OR number to open the receipt and print it again.

10 Balance the cash at the end of the day

Reports opens the Daily collection report: every receipt of the day, cash in, cash out, and your total. End-of-day close groups the same receipts by type with the cash-box grand total; print it and count the box against it.

Role guide · sign in as loanofficer

Loan Officer

You can

  • File loan applications; edit or cancel them while pending
  • Check a member's eligibility; edit member profiles
  • Update the delinquency list and print demand letters
  • Generate reminders, send them by SMS or email, mark them sent or failed, remove them
  • Open the Loan status and Delinquency & aging reports

You cannot

  • Approve, reject, or release a loan (Manager)
  • Register members, or post deposits, withdrawals, or payments (Cashier)
  • Open the End-of-day close, Reconciliation, the Audit Log, Savings Interest, or User Accounts

1 Start from the dashboard

Applications in process, past-due loans, and installments due in 7 days come first, with a delinquency summary of the worst accounts.

2 Find the borrowing member

Loans → New application (or New loan on an active member's record). Type the name or member number and pick the member.

3 Encode the application

Choose the product, enter the principal and term, pick the co-maker from the member list, and fill in the collateral (needed for a Regular Loan and for members outside the school) or the net pay (Salary Loan). The eligibility summary beside the form tells you what is still required.

Below the form, the amortization preview updates as you type. Show it to the member before clicking Submit application.

4 Follow, edit, or cancel the pending application

The application is now Pending and waits for the credit committee and the Manager. Until it is decided you can Edit it or Cancel application (a reason is asked). Loans lists every loan by status.

5 Update the delinquency list

Delinquency → Update list now. The system scans every schedule and lists the past-due accounts by aging bracket, with the contact number and the co-maker.

6 Print a demand letter

Click Demand letter on the past-due account, then Print letter. The co-maker gets a copy.

7 Send the payment reminders

Reminders → Generate reminders. For each reminder, open it in the SMS app (phone button) or the email app (envelope), then mark it sent (✓) or failed (✕), or remove it (bin). Reminders for members with an email address are also sent automatically when the Manager has set up the email account.

8 Open your reports

Reports shows two tabs for the Loan Officer: Loan status and Delinquency & aging. Both can be exported or printed.

Role guide · sign in as bookkeeper

Bookkeeper

You can

  • Post the savings interest (quarterly, and per term for time deposits)
  • Reverse a savings entry, with a reason
  • Void the latest receipt of a loan, with a reason
  • Reopen a closed savings account
  • Open the Daily collection, Savings, Share capital, and Salary deduction reports
  • Open the End-of-day close, Reconciliation, the Audit Log, and the Delinquency list

You cannot

  • Post deposits, withdrawals, or payments (Cashier)
  • Register or edit members; file, approve, or release loans
  • Close an account (Manager)
  • Open Reminders or User Accounts

1 Start from the dashboard

2 Post the savings interest

Savings Interest. Type the quarter label and click Post quarterly interest: every active regular savings account with a balance is credited 1% in one run, and accounts already posted for that label are skipped. For a time deposit, type its term label on its own row and click Post.

3 Reverse a wrong savings entry

Open the passbook (Savings & Share Capital → Passbook) and click the red ↺ button on the wrong line.

Type the reason and click Reverse entry. A correcting line is added; the original stays, greyed out.

4 Void a wrong loan receipt

Open the loan (Loans, or click the loan from Payments). In the Payments list, the latest posted receipt has a Void button.

Type the reason and click Void receipt.

The installments and the balance are restored, and the receipt stays on record marked VOID. To void an older receipt, void the newer ones first.

5 Reopen a closed account

On the passbook of a closed account, click Reopen account.

6 Check the day: end-of-day close

End-of-day close. Pick the business date. A reversal you made appears as its own Savings reversal line. The Daily collection report (Reports) lists the same receipts one by one.

7 Prepare the reports

Reports → Savings gives the movements and the balances per member; Share capital gives each member's paid-up capital; Salary deduction list gives what to deduct on the 15th and the 30th for the chosen payroll month. Export to Excel/CSV or print.

8 Verify: reconciliation and audit log

Reconciliation checks every stored balance against its ledger; anything that does not match is listed in red. The Audit Log shows who did what and when, including your voids and reversals with their reasons.

Role guide · sign in as auditor

Board / Auditor

You can

  • Open every member record, passbook, loan, payment, and receipt
  • Open all six reports, and export or print them
  • Open the End-of-day close, Reconciliation, the Delinquency list, and the Audit Log
  • Read the loan products and the settings

You cannot

  • Add, change, post, approve, void, or reverse anything: the role is read-only
  • Open Reminders, Savings Interest, or User Accounts

1 Start from the dashboard

2 Inspect any record

Open Members, Savings & Share Capital, Loans, or Payments, or press Ctrl+K to find a member. Every record opens in full, but with no buttons to change it.

3 Read the reports

Reports shows all six tabs: Daily collection, Loan status, Delinquency & aging, Savings, Share capital, and the Salary deduction list.

4 Check the books: reconciliation and audit log

Reconciliation proves that the stored balances agree with the ledgers. The Audit Log lists every sign-in, entry, approval, correction, and settings change with the user and the time; filter it by date, user, or record type.

5 What happens on a page you may not open

A page outside the role answers Access denied, even when its address is typed directly. The same applies to every role.

Development process

How the system was built: requirements, decisions, build order, security, testing, and installation.

Section B.1

Requirements sources

Section B.6

Installation

  1. Copy the mutuallink folder to C:\wamp64\www\mutuallink.
  2. Create the credentials file: copy config/secrets.example.php to config/secrets.php and set the database user/password (it is gitignored — never commit it).
  3. Open phpMyAdmin as root → Import → database/mutuallink.sql, then change the app account's password and match it in config/secrets.php (README §1).
  4. Open http://localhost/mutuallink and sign in. This guide is at http://localhost/mutuallink/documentation/.
  5. Backups: edit the MySQL path in database/backup.bat and double-click it (or schedule it in Task Scheduler). Restore with mysql -u root -p mutuallink < database\backups\mutuallink_YYYYMMDD_HHMM.sql.
  6. Email reminders: in Reminders → Email settings fill in the SMTP account (Gmail: App Password, see A.6), test once by hand, then schedule cron_send_reminders.php every 10 minutes in Task Scheduler (README §6 has the exact steps).
